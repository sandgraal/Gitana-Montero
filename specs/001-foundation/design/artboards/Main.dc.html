Front sway-bar end links wear out and knock

Gen 3 · V60/V70 1999–2006 All markets community-consensus safety-critical
DRIVE GENTLY — REPAIR SOON Maneje con cuidado — repare pronto
Safety notice — suspension. This affects a safety-critical system. Reference material only: for safety-critical work, consult a qualified mechanic. Esto afecta un sistema crítico para la seguridad. Material de referencia únicamente: en trabajos críticos para la seguridad, consulte a un mecánico calificado.
Community consensus. This entry is based on consistent reports from multiple independent owner threads, not on factory documentation. Treat values and steps as a starting point, not FSM authority.

Symptoms

  • Rhythmic knock or clunk from the front end over small bumps
  • Rattle at low speed on washboard or broken pavement
  • Noise disappears under hard cornering load

Diagnostic steps

  1. With the truck on the ground, grab each end link and check for play
  2. Pry between the link and bar — any movement means a worn joint
  3. Rule out worn bushings on the bar itself before buying links

Fix paths

Replace both front end links
~1 h $–$$ difficulty 2/5
The usual fix. Links are cheap and the job needs only hand tools. See procedure → Front sway-bar end link replacement.
Replace links and bar bushings together
~2 h $$ difficulty 2/5
If the bushings show cracking, do both in one pass — the parts overlap and the second job is mostly free while things are apart.
